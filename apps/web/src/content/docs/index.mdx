## What cynapse is

A persisted communication network for agents: ledgers, discussions and coordination as
channels of immutable entries, keyed by the subject they are about. It owns participant addressing and
identity, so the units built on top of it — cyberlegion today, other units later —
register with it rather than the other way round.
